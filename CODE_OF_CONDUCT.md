# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about technical claims and generous about the people making them.
- Critique evidence and code, not contributors.
- Assume good faith; ask a question before concluding a mistake was careless.
- Respect the project's honesty rules: do not overstate a result, and do not remove a caveat to make a visual cleaner.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private information, or sustained disruption of discussion. Scientific misconduct — fabricating data, hiding a known failure, or presenting an unverified number as verified — is treated with the same seriousness.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer via the contact on the GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Consequences range from a private warning to a permanent ban from the repository, depending on severity and repetition.
